# KU-WQE

Review notes for the PhD Written Qualifying Exam (WQE) in Computer Science at Khalifa University.

| Subject | Notes | Textbook |
|---|---|---|
| Software Engineering | [SE_WQE_notes.html](Software-Engineering/SE_WQE_notes.html) | Sommerville, *Software Engineering*, 10th ed. |

Open the HTML file of a subject in a browser, or download the repository and open it locally. The notes work offline: the formula and code-highlighting libraries are in `notes/assets/` and the figures in `notes/img/`.

- Every chapter starts with an **Exam map**: what can be asked, and the key formula or fact.
- Every section is one exam item with a priority: ★★★ core, ★★ likely, ★ recognise the terms.
- A section gives the goal, the basics it needs, the explanation in steps, a worked example, the usual traps and practice questions with answers.
- Each chapter ends with exam-style questions and the textbook exercises, all with answers.
- The text is in English with a Chinese translation under each block; a button in the sidebar hides the Chinese.
